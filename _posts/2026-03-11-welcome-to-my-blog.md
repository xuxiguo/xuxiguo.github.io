Welcome to my new blog! I'll be sharing weekly thoughts on artificial intelligence, machine learning in finance, and other topics at the intersection of technology and investment research.

## What to Expect

I plan to cover topics including:

- **AI in Finance**: How machine learning is transforming hedge funds, mutual funds, and financial analysis
- **Research Updates**: Insights from my ongoing research projects
- **Industry Trends**: Commentary on the latest developments in FinTech and AI
- **Teaching Reflections**: Observations from my blockchain and finance courses

## Why Blog?

As an academic researcher, I believe in making knowledge accessible. While academic papers serve an important purpose, they often take years to publish and can be dense for general audiences. This blog is my way of sharing timely insights and engaging with a broader community.

Stay tuned for weekly posts!
